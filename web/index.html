How a round works

  1. Pick a side. RED (Onshore: TSLA, SPCX, COIN, PLTR, USO, USAR) or BLUE (Open trade: TSM, ASML, AAPL, AMZN, BABA, EWY).
  2. Call the spread. How far RED's return lands above or below BLUE's, in basis points. You sign it in your wallet: free, no gas.
  3. Lock. Picks close two minutes before the lock. Every pick is recorded on Robinhood Chain before the lock; nothing can be added after it.
  4. Score. Scored from Chainlink prices on Robinhood Chain. 100 points for the right side, up to 100 more for how close your spread is. The top callers of each round win prizes from the prize wallet (stock tokens when it holds them, otherwise ETH).
